Personal Introduction

Hello, I'm Mingfan Zhao(赵明帆), a Ph.D. candidate at Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Dr. Wen-Dong Mao and Prof. Zhongfeng Wang (IEEE Fellow). I received my M.S. from Xi'an Institute of Optics and Precision Mechanics, CAS in 2023 under Prof. Xiaoqiang Lu, and my B.S. from Northwest University (China) in 2018. My research interests include Deep Learning, AI Hardware Acceleration, and Robotics.

Publications

DATA: A Memory-Efficient Deformable Transformer Accelerator via Neural Architecture Search
M. Zhao, W. Mao, Z. Wang
IEEE Transactions on Very Large Scale Integration (VLSI) Systems, vol. 33, no. 11, pp. 3097-3108, Nov. 2025
First Author
SCI Journal Paper

This paper presents DATA, a memory-efficient deformable transformer accelerator designed through neural architecture search. The work focuses on optimizing hardware implementation of transformer models with deformable attention mechanisms, addressing key challenges in memory management and computational efficiency.

DOI: 10.1109/TVLSI.2025.3589359
